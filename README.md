One wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding — a single sentence, 140 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time:

> I vow to love you through every update, every crash, and every low battery, and to keep holding your hand until my last circuit powers down.

The laugh comes from the situation itself: a machine reciting the classic "for better or worse" in its own terms — updates, crashes, low battery. The tenderness lands at the end, holding hands until the last circuit powers down.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

That writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network and no model call.
